About Tigers

Tigers are the largest living cat species and are known for their distinctive orange coats with black stripes.

They aren't known for their distinctive manes, strong social behaviour, and powerful roar.

Tigers are mainly found in parts of Asia, with a small population of Siberian tigers in Russia.

Quick Facts

  • Social group: pride
  • Diet: carnivore
  • Known for: roar and mane
